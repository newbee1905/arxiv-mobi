      What this fixes

      • Type sized for a hand, not a monitor. Left-aligned by default — justified text in a 45-character column is all rivers and gaps. Size, line spacing, serif/sans and justification are all adjustable.
      • Tables that survive dark mode. arXiv passes the authors' literal colours through, so "dark mode" grows blinding white rows. Every baked-in colour is remapped into the theme, hue kept, contrast checked.
      • Figures open full screen. Tap any figure for pinch, double-tap and wheel zoom, swipe to the next one, caption in reach, invert for dark rooms.
      • A dark theme modelled on sioyek. Deep neutral ground, ink dimmed to stop the glow, amber marks — plus a true-black variant for OLED and a sepia one for lamp light.
      • Wide equations and tables fit. They are scaled down to the column where possible and scroll sideways when not, with the equation number pinned in view.
      • Citations peek instead of teleport. Tapping [17] shows the reference where you are; footnotes and figure references too.
      • Works without a connection. Install it to the home screen, save papers with their figures, and pick up at the paragraph you left.
      • No server, no accounts, no tracking. Papers are fetched straight from arXiv by your browser; preferences and reading positions never leave the device.

      Fast ways in

      Swap the host. Any arXiv address works if you put this site in front of it: …/abs/2401.12345 opens the reader directly.

      Bookmarklet. Drag this to your bookmarks bar, then press it on any arXiv page:
      Read in arxiv-mobi

      Share sheet. Install this site as an app and arXiv links can be shared straight into it from any other app.

      Install. Use your browser's Add to Home Screen to get a standalone reader with offline papers.
